# LIFEWS Pathways™ App

**From Learning to Opportunity.**

LIFEWS Pathways™ is a digital education-to-opportunity platform designed to support Almajiri learners, out-of-school girls, underserved adolescents, and transitioning young adults through structured learning, practical skills development, agriculture, food literacy, digital competencies, safeguarding, and verified transition pathways.

The platform operationalizes the LIFEWS Pathways™ program through five developmental stages: **Discover™**, **Explore™**, **Build™**, **Transition™**, and **Enterprise™**.

## Core capabilities

- Learner enrollment and baseline assessment
- Attendance and participation tracking
- Age-banded curriculum delivery
- GrowMeal™ garden learning activities
- Food Discovery Lab™ activities
- SkillsBridge™ practical skills modules
- Pathways Passport™ records
- Badges and competency evidence
- Safeguarding workflows and restricted incident records
- Transition planning and verified destination tracking
- AgriRoots™, AgriNext™, and AgriAble™ referrals
- Adult-only ZARIKS™ Enterprise Bridge
- KPI and program dashboards
- Mobile-first, low-connectivity operation
- Role-based access for administrators, educators, safeguarding officers, transition officers, M&E staff, community coordinators, and approved partners

## Program rule

Children are learners first. Participation in LIFEWS Pathways™ is never conditional upon purchasing, consuming, promoting, or selling ZARIKS™ or any other commercial product. Commercial pathways are available only to eligible adults and remain voluntary and institutionally separate from child programming.

## Initial technical direction

The first implementation uses a modern TypeScript web stack with a mobile-first responsive interface and a backend designed for secure role-based access, offline-tolerant field workflows, and structured program data.

Development work is staged through feature branches and pull requests before merging into `main`.
